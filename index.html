🔬 Research Interests研究方向

My research interests include large language models, data mining, reinforcement learning, and financial technology. I am particularly interested in building intelligent systems that can reason, adapt, and make reliable decisions in complex and dynamic environments.

我的研究方向包括大语言模型、数据挖掘、强化学习与金融科技，重点关注如何构建能够在复杂动态环境中进行推理、适应并作出可靠决策的智能系统。
